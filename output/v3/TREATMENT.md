# v3: "The Seed of Light", treatment and storyboard

- Their expressions: laughter, hair flips, the hand-to-face giggles and the embrace.

## Concept
The wall behind the two friends is a sheet of paper, and an invisible hand draws a small world onto it in ink.
- **Ink, not stickers.** Fine navy ink lines are multiplied into the wall, so they take on its light and texture, plus soft watercolour washes in a restrained palette. Only things that really emit light glow: windows, the seed, pollen, lanterns and stars.
- **Behind the performers.** The world sits behind the two friends, so they pass in front of it. When the little characters walk behind a head, they disappear and come out the other side.
- **A parallel story.** Two small ink characters live out a friendship story that mirrors the real one.

**Palette:**

| Element | Colour |
|---|---|
| Ink lines | Navy |
| Distant hills | Muted lavender |
| Wanderer, west cottage roof, blossoms | Warm coral |
| Friend, east cottage roof | Deep teal |
| Leaves | Emerald |
| Light (the motif that connects everything) | Gold |
| Character faces | Cream |

Each colour belongs to one character or motif.

**Recurring motif, the light:** falling seed → glowing sapling → pollen that makes flowers bloom → lanterns on a string → rising lanterns → stars → a constellation in the shape of a heart.

## Geography
All positions are fixed, so continuity is guaranteed.
- **West:** a coral-roofed cottage on the left (the Wanderer's home).
- **Valley:** the open wall between the two heads, where the tree grows.
- **East:** a teal-roofed cottage on the right (the Friend's home).
- **The path:** one winding ink road links the three. It passes behind both heads.
- **Distance:** lavender hills and slow-drifting cloud outlines sit along the top. A moon and stars appear at night.

## Rules
- **Faces stay clear:** the illustrated world never covers a face, an eye or a mouth. Everything is composited behind the person mask, which is unioned with detected face boxes.
- **Graphics with a reason:** the only graphics in front of the performers are the gold particles released by their own hand gestures. These are masked off faces.
- **No mechanical beat effects:** nothing fires on every beat. Beats are used for authored story moments: growth steps, lanterns lighting, light pulses, the greeting jump.
- **Camera:** a slow, breathing push-in, with no zoom pulses.
- **Colour grade:**
  - filmic curve with a soft shoulder
  - warm ivory paper-white wall
  - slightly cool, never crushed, shadows
  - rich but controlled maroon
  - natural skin with only a very light softening
  - halation on highlights
  - fine luma-only grain
  - a gentle vignette
