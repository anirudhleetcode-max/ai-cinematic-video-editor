# Asset credits: `cute_doodle_music_edit_v2.mp4`

## External assets

None. No external video or image assets were used.

The request asked for doodle-art clips from Pixabay (https://pixabay.com/videos/search/doodle%20art/). This render environment's network policy blocks Pixabay: every attempt to reach `pixabay.com` and `cdn.pixabay.com` was refused by the egress proxy with HTTP 403 (checked on 2026-10-20). No Pixabay asset was downloaded, previewed or used. As the brief allowed, every doodle was drawn procedurally instead.

To use Pixabay clips in a later version, allow `pixabay.com` and `cdn.pixabay.com` in the environment's network settings, then credit each clip here with its title, page URL and creator.
