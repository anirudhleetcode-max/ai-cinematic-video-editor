## Known limitations
- **No lip-sync:** the girls sing a different song, so their mouths don't match this music.
- **Fast motion:** fast arms and hair are motion-blurred in the source, and the cut-out keeps that blur.
- **Whip pans:** for one or two frames a whip shows the edge of the outgoing frame, by design.

## Reproduce

